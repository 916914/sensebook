The product

The book stays the screen.

Illustration, not a photo or render. Unit about 8.5 × 3.8 × 3 cm (target).

Clip it on

Two thin blades slip inside the covers, a third leg rests on the spine. Nothing touches the pages.

Turn the page

It sees the spread, waits for the page to settle and carries on. No buttons.

Listen

The reader voice reads your textbook from verified text, and says when it isn't sure.

Ask and practise

Hold Ask: after a bell, the explainer voice helps. Then it quizzes you and plans your revision.

Bangla and English · speaker, wired headphones or Bluetooth earbuds · downloaded textbooks read without internet.

Product spec v1.1; tri-grip attachment plan. Prototype software runs on a laptop; hardware not yet built.SenseBook · 3 / 10

The evidence, honestly

Change, then test. Don't tool hardware yet.

Our own desk validation (about 390 dated sources) found a real need and unproven hard parts. So the launch promise is narrow: your own textbook, read from verified text, explained and quizzed. Three cheap tests come before any pilot money.

1 · The phone test

10 students try SenseBook and a phone with free AI on the same stand.

Pass: ≥60% keep SenseBook; ≥30% of parents place a deposit. ~Tk 40,000, 2 weeks.

2 · The Bangla reading test

30 pages at the real camera view; voices; children's spoken questions.

Pass: ≤2% character error, ≤1 invented word per 1,000. ~Tk 5,000–10,000.

3 · Blind readers and sponsors

8 blind and low-vision readers attach it and read alone; 10 funders asked.

Pass: ≥80% in ≤15 s; ≥2 letters of intent. ~Tk 20,000, 4–6 weeks.

Also fixed first: the AI provider must allow use by under-18s; NCTB permission for textbook content; the 2026 data law's guardian consent. Costs are estimates.
