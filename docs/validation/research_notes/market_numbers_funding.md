# Market size, price, unit cost, volumes and funding: published data check (Bangladesh, as of 27 Nov 2026)

*Method note: public pages and search only. "[page]" means I opened the page and read the figure there. "[snippet]" means the figure comes from a search-engine summary of that URL and I did not open the page, so check it before quoting. FX used for conversions: Tk 124.35 per US$ (Bangladesh Bank weighted average, 8 Nov 2026). All calculations in "Inferences" are mine.*

## 1. Enrolment: secondary students (Class 6–10), school types, urban/rural, Dakhil

### Takeaway
The plan's 8.64 million is a real BANBEIS 2025 figure, but it covers general secondary schools, school-and-colleges and a few primary schools that teach up to Class 8. It does **not** include madrasas (2.84 million), English-medium schools or technical schools, so the plan's label "all types incl. madrasa, English medium" is wrong as reported. Enrolment is also falling: down about 425,000 in one year and down for the third year in a row. BANBEIS publishes no urban/rural split of students.

### Cited Findings
- BANBEIS *Bangladesh Education Statistics 2025* (published June 2026): secondary-level enrolment was "more than 8.638 million" in 2025, down from "more than 9.063 million" in 2024. — [Prothom Alo English, updated 15 Oct 2026](https://en.prothomalo.com/youth/education/f8t5oc4rxz) [page]
- How the 8.638 million breaks down: 7.439 million in 19,211 secondary schools (junior schools included), 1.128 million at secondary level in 1,556 school-and-college institutions, and about 70,000 in 547 government primary schools that teach up to Class 8. The article does not say the total includes madrasa, English-medium or technical students. — [Prothom Alo English](https://en.prothomalo.com/youth/education/f8t5oc4rxz) [page]
- Secondary-school enrolment alone fell from 7,847,546 (2024) to 7,439,000 (2025), even though 94 more schools opened. — [Prothom Alo English](https://en.prothomalo.com/youth/education/f8t5oc4rxz) [page]
- Madrasas (Dakhil, Alim, Fazil and Kamil together, 9,296 institutions): "more than 2.84 million" students in 2025, up from 2.796 million. This excludes independent Ebtedayee and Qawmi madrasas. **No Dakhil-only (Class 6–10 equivalent) figure is given.** — [Prothom Alo English](https://en.prothomalo.com/youth/education/f8t5oc4rxz) [page]
- English-medium: 143 schools with 29,672 students in 2025, up from 29,077 in 2024. — [Prothom Alo English](https://en.prothomalo.com/youth/education/f8t5oc4rxz) [page]
- Independent technical and vocational institutions: 650,000 students in 2025, down from more than 676,000. — [Prothom Alo English](https://en.prothomalo.com/youth/education/f8t5oc4rxz) [page]
- Other 2025 totals in the press do not match: Bonik Barta reports 9,405,785 secondary-level students, and Prothom Alo elsewhere gives about 9.8 million across general, madrasa, English-medium and technical. The bases are not explained. — [Bonik Barta English](https://en.bonikbarta.com/bangladesh/JhDMeK0yiyDtEmOZ) [snippet]
- Secondary net enrolment rate fell to 65.1% in 2025 from 70.02% in 2024 (BANBEIS). — [Daily Sun](https://www.daily-sun.com/bangladesh/904738) [snippet]
- Secondary-school student numbers fell for the third year in a row. — [The Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/node/3577171) [snippet, headline only]
- Population proxy for an urban split: Census 2022 final adjusted figures put 31.66% of the population (53.76 million) in urban areas and 68.34% in rural areas. — [Dhaka Tribune, "68% Bangladeshis live in villages"](https://www.dhakatribune.com/amp/bangladesh/332419/report-68%25-bangladeshis-live-in-villages) [snippet]

### Inferences
- Counting all school types: 8.64M + madrasa 2.84M (all madrasa levels, so too high for Dakhil alone) + English-medium 0.03M + technical 0.65M ≈ 12.2M. That is an upper bound. The Dakhil-only figure is somewhere below 2.84M. The plan should either relabel 8.64M as "general secondary only" or add a sourced Dakhil figure.
- The plan's ~30% urban share is close to the 31.66% census population share. But the urban share of *students* may differ, and BANBEIS does not publish it. Treat 30% as an estimate.
- Enrolment is falling (about −4.7% in one year), so a TAM held flat for Years 2–3 slightly overstates the market.
- English-medium students (29,672) are probably the richest segment. As a count it is too small to change the TAM. It may be undercounted, since many English-medium schools are not registered, but that is not confirmed.

### Gaps
- No BANBEIS urban/rural split of Class 6–10 students was found. The full BANBEIS 2025 PDF (banbeis.gov.bd) may have upazila or district tables that could be grouped. Not checked.
- No Dakhil-level (Class 6–10) madrasa enrolment figure was found.
- I could not confirm from the article that BANBEIS "secondary" here means exactly Classes 6–10.

## 2. Income and affordability: HIES 2022, quintiles/deciles, inflation 2023–2026, real income, poverty

### Takeaway
HIES 2022 average monthly household income was Tk 32,422 nationally, Tk 45,757 urban and Tk 26,163 rural. Income is highly concentrated: the top 10% of households earn 40.9% of all income. Inflation stayed high (10.03% in FY25, 8.68% in FY26, 8.27% in Oct 2026) and is still above wage growth (7.63% in Oct 2026), so real wages are still falling. The World Bank estimates poverty has risen from 18.7% (2022) to about 22.5% (FY26). The press did not report quintile income levels in Tk, and no source gives the share of households that can pay Tk 15,000 at once.

### Cited Findings
- HIES 2022 average monthly household income (nominal): urban Tk 45,757 (Tk 22,600 in 2016), rural Tk 26,163 (Tk 13,398 in 2016). — [The Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/business/news/household-income-doubles-6-years-so-does-spending-3497661) [snippet]
- National average Tk 32,422 (Tk 15,988 in 2016). Per-capita monthly income Tk 7,614. — [The Financial Express](https://thefinancialexpress.com.bd/economy/bangladesh/poverty-falls-faster-to-187pc-income-inequality-gapes); [TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/node/615386) [snippet]
- Income Gini 0.499 nationally (0.482 in 2016), 0.539 urban, 0.446 rural. — [The Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/business/news/household-income-doubles-6-years-so-does-spending-3497661) [snippet]
- Top 10% of households earned 40.91% of income in 2022 (38.09% in 2016). Decile 5 earned 5.81% and decile 6 earned 6.92%, both down from 2016. "Decile 1" earned 0.37%, but one outlet labels that as the bottom 5%, so the label is disputed. — [Prothom Alo English](https://en.prothomalo.com/business/local/i9wd0lg6hk); [The Financial Express](https://thefinancialexpress.com.bd/economy/inequality-increases-as-50pc-amass-304pc-wealth) [snippet]
- Bottom 20% income share rose 0.33 pp from 3.85% (2016), which implies about 4.2% in 2022. Middle 40% share fell 2.21 pp. Top-10% to bottom-50% income ratio rose to 2.2 from 2.0. The 50th-percentile per-capita income is about 42% below the national mean. — [TBS, Zahid Hussain, 2 Aug 2023](https://www.tbsnews.net/analysis/tale-two-tails-675902?amp) [page]
- Inflation: FY2024-25 average 10.03%. FY2025-26 average 8.68%, missing the 7% revised target. FY2026-27 target is 7.5%. — [The Financial Express](https://thefinancialexpress.com.bd/economy/average-annual-inflation-cools-to-868-per-cent-in-fy26); [Bonik Barta](https://en.bonikbarta.com/bangladesh/Zzfum6iKrKhxDkMK) [snippet]
- October 2026 point-to-point inflation was 8.27% (8.34% in Sept). Food 6.40%, non-food 9.89% (another outlet gives non-food 9.48%). The 12-month average for Nov 2025–Oct 2026 was 8.66%. Wage growth fell to 7.63% in Oct from 7.9% in Sept. — [Daily Sun, 9 Nov 2026](https://www.daily-sun.com/economy/908166/inflation-eases-slightly-to-8-27-in-oct); [Bonik Barta](https://en.bonikbarta.com/bangladesh/EU6HWCWdGjAT27ib/) [snippet]
- Poverty: 18.7% in 2022 (HIES), down from 37.1% in 2010, and progress has since reversed. — [World Bank press release, 25 Nov 2025](https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2025/11/25/job-creation-key-to-equality-and-faster-poverty-reduction-in-bangladesh-says-world-bank) [snippet]
- World Bank estimates (micro-simulations, not a new survey): 21.4% in 2025 (Apr 2026 Development Update) and about 22.5% in FY26, with 2.1M more people below the line (Oct 2026 update). — [World Bank, 8 Apr 2026](https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2026/04/08/urgent-reforms-needed-to-restore-macro-stability-sustain-growth-and-create-jobs-in-bangladesh); [The Financial Express](https://thefinancialexpress.com.bd/economy/21m-more-fell-below-poverty-line-in-fy26) [snippet]
- PPRC's private survey put poverty at 27.93% in 2025, using different poverty lines. — [Prothom Alo English](https://en.prothomalo.com/amp/story/business/local/qvnxuyzl7k) [snippet]

### Inferences
- Average income of the top decile in 2022 ≈ 40.91% × 10 × Tk 32,422 ≈ **Tk 132,600 per month** (nominal, national ranking, assuming equal household counts per decile). Decile 6 average ≈ 6.92% × 10 × 32,422 ≈ Tk 22,400. These are rough because the decile labels in the press are inconsistent.
- The device (Tk 14,990) costs about 33% of one month's average urban household income in 2022, about 46% of the national average, and about 11% of a top-decile month. The subscription (Tk 399) is about 0.9% of average urban monthly income.
- Prices rose about 19.6% over FY25 and FY26 combined (1.1003 × 1.0868), and wage growth is below inflation. 2022 incomes in today's money are lower in real terms than the HIES headline suggests. Any nominal update of HIES incomes to 2026 is an estimate.
- "Half of urban students in the top two national quintiles" is plausible, because urban mean income is about 1.41× the national mean. It is not confirmed: no source gives the urban share of households in quintiles 4–5.

### Gaps
- No Tk values for quintile or decile incomes, and no urban-specific decile tables. These are in the BBS HIES 2022 final report tables, but I did not extract the PDF ([Khulna portal PDF](https://file-khulna.portal.gov.bd/uploads/3eaa290c-fbed-454c-8f7d-3512306d5e45//659/23d/0c1/65923d0c15851326838909.pdf)). The CEIC series page returned 403.
- No published share of households that could make a Tk 15,000 one-off purchase.
- Average inflation for FY2022-23 and FY2023-24 was not retrieved in this pass.

## 3. Internet: household internet access and smartphone ownership, urban vs rural

### Takeaway
The latest BBS ICT Access and Use Survey (2025-26, quarterly) puts household internet access at 57–58% nationally and household smartphone ownership at 73–75%. The latest urban/rural split I found is from 2024: about 61.6% of urban and 48.2% of rural households had internet. The plan's 76% urban figure is higher than BBS's urban household internet figure. It may be measuring something else, such as smartphones.

### Cited Findings
- BBS ICT Access and Use Survey 2025-26, household internet access: 55.1% (2024-25), then 56.2% (Q1, Jul–Sep 2025), 57.2% (Q2), 57.4% (Q3, Jan–Mar 2026). Household smartphone ownership: 72.7%, 72.4%, 73.0%, 73.4%. Household mobile phone ownership 98.9%. Computer ownership 9%. — [The Daily Star, 5 Jul 2026](https://www.thedailystar.net/business/news/internet-use-climbs-steadily-q3-4215836) [page]
- Individuals aged 5+, Q3 (Jan–Mar 2026): 58.6% used the internet, 65.4% owned a mobile phone, 89.5% used one, 11.7% used a computer. — [The Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/business/news/internet-use-climbs-steadily-q3-4215836) [page]
- Q4 (Apr–Jun 2026): household internet access 58.2%, slightly below 59.7% a year earlier. Individual internet use 59.2%. One report gives household smartphone ownership as 75.1%. — [Dhaka Tribune](https://www.dhakatribune.com/bangladesh/415972/bbs-survey-over-57%25-of-bangladeshi-households-now); [Bonik Barta](https://en.bonikbarta.com/bangladesh/RCHaIHkU7QGCUrPr) [snippet; figures differ between outlets]
- 2024 quarterly data: about 61.6% of urban households and 48.2% of rural households used the internet. — [TBS, "52% households have internet connections"](https://www.tbsnews.net/tech/52-households-bangladesh-have-internet-connections-bbs-1114086) [snippet; I did not confirm that this is the exact article]
- Sample: 61,632 households per quarter, 2,568 sample areas, all 64 districts. — [Dhaka Tribune](https://www.dhakatribune.com/bangladesh/415972/bbs-survey-over-57%25-of-bangladeshi-households-now) [snippet]

### Inferences
- Using BBS urban household internet (about 61.6%) instead of 76%: 8.64M × 30% × 50% × 61.6% ≈ **0.80M** serviceable students, against the plan's ~1.0M. Higher-income urban households probably have above-average internet access, so the truth is likely between 0.8M and 1.0M. That is not confirmed.
- Household smartphone ownership (73–75% nationally) is higher than household internet access (57–58%). The device's setup or subscription relies on internet in the home, so "has internet" is the right filter, not "has a smartphone".

### Gaps
- No urban/rural split was found for the 2025-26 ICT survey quarters. The BBS quarterly reports themselves (bbs.gov.bd) are the place to look.
- No figure was found for internet access among households with school-age children.

## 5. Subscription benchmarks (device + subscription) and exam seasonality

### Takeaway
Hard published attach or retention data for device-plus-subscription edtech is scarce. Peloton (premium, US) shows 1.2–2.2% monthly churn, about 77–86% kept per year. No published churn data was found for Youdao, Kindle Unlimited, Amazon Kids+, Byju's tablets, Shikho or 10 Minute School. The plan's 45% attach and 60% annual retention are unbenchmarked. The 2027 SSC exam runs 14 March to 15 April, moved from January.

### Cited Findings
- Peloton average net monthly paid Connected Fitness churn: 1.6% (Q1 FY26), 1.2% (Q3 FY26), 2.2% (Q4 FY26, against 1.8% in Q4 FY25). Ending subscriptions 2.553M, down 8.8% year on year. Q1 FY27 churn 1.7%. — [Peloton Q4 FY2026 release (SEC 8-K)](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001639825/000163982526000032/q4fy2026earningspressrelea.htm); [Peloton Q2 FY2026](https://investor.onepeloton.com/news-releases/news-release-details/peloton-announces-q2-fy2026-financial-results) [snippet]
- Youdao smart devices: FY2025 net revenue RMB 739.6M (US$105.8M), down 18.2%. Gross margin was 50.3% in Q3 2025 (up from 42.8%, helped by the new Dictionary Pen) and 38.1% in Q4 2025 (down from 43.9%, "increased bill of materials cost"). The Dictionary Pen topped JD/Tmall at Singles' Day for the 6th year running. — [Youdao Q4/FY2025 results (Barchart)](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/149225/youdao-reports-fourth-quarter-and-fiscal-year-2025-unaudited-financial-results); [Youdao Q3 2025 (Barchart)](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/36222973/youdao-reports-third-quarter-2025-unaudited-financial-results) [snippet]
- Byju's tablet programme, Class 9 tablet with recorded classes: list ₹29,990, sale ₹26,000. The SD-card course on Flipkart shows 1-year subscription validity. A 2023 refund annexure lists a ₹6,000 device charge and a ₹7,000 registration fee. — [Byju's shop](https://shop.byjus.com/products/byju-s-the-learning-app-with-learnstation-class-9); [Flipkart listing](https://www.flipkart.com/byju-s-class-11th-pcm-jee-course/p/itmedw3vyt9vf5mh) [snippet]
- SSC 2027 (nine general boards): written exams 14 March to 15 April 2027, practicals by 22 April. It was originally 7 January to 6 February, and SSC was traditionally held in early February. HSC runs 6 June to 13 July 2027. Routine published 24 Aug 2026. — [Dhaka Tribune](https://www.dhakatribune.com/bangladesh/education/418217/2027-ssc-exams-rescheduled-to-start-march-14); [Jago News](https://www.jagonews24.com/en/education/news/95459); [The Daily Star](https://online-d11.thedailystar.net/news/education/news/ssc-exams-january-7-hsc-june-6-2027-4175666) [snippet]

### Inferences
- Peloton's 1.6% average monthly churn means roughly 82% of subscribers stay each year ((1−0.016)^12). That is for a US$1,000+ product where the buyer has already sunk a lot of money. A Tk 399 add-on to a Tk 15,000 device is likely to churn faster. The plan's 60% annual retention may be reasonable or optimistic; there is no regional benchmark to test it.
- Youdao's Q4 2025 margin drop from rising BOM costs is a live example of 2025–26 memory and component inflation hitting a learning-device maker.
- Demand will likely cluster around the January school-year start and the months before SSC (now March–April 2027), with possible churn after exams. This is not measured.

### Gaps
- No public attach or retention rates were found for Youdao subscriptions, Kindle Unlimited, Amazon Kids+ or Byju's tablet renewals.
- No churn or paid-subscriber data was found for Shikho or 10 Minute School (one search returned only an unrelated Indian app, Seekho).

## 6. Customer acquisition: Meta ad costs, COD refusal and returns, courier costs

### Takeaway
No reliable Bangladesh-specific Meta CPM, CPC or cost per purchase was found; only an unsourced blog range ($0.20–0.60 CPM). COD dominates e-commerce (about 90% of users prefer it, per e-CAB), and return-to-origin rates of 20–30% are quoted only by vendor blogs. Courier delivery inside Dhaka starts at about Tk 60–65 plus a COD fee of 0.5–1%.

### Cited Findings
- Blog estimate: Bangladesh Facebook CPM about $0.20–0.60 and CPC about $0.03–0.10 in 2025. No data source is given. — [mycpmcalculator blog](https://mycpmcalculator.netlify.app/blog/cpm-rates-by-country) [snippet]
- Global averages for comparison (Nov 2025): Facebook CPC $0.87, CPM $16.06. — [Shopify](https://shopify.com/blog/facebook-ads-cost) [snippet]
- e-CAB research: more than 90% of e-commerce users prefer cash on delivery. A market-data firm estimates COD at 75% of e-commerce volume. — [US trade.gov Country Commercial Guide](https://trade.gov/country-commercial-guides/bangladesh-ecommerce); [PaymentsCMI](https://paymentscmi.com/insights/bangladesh-ecommerce-market-insights/) [snippet; attribution of the e-CAB figure to trade.gov not confirmed]
- Vendor blog estimate: 20–30% return-to-origin on COD orders in Bangladesh, no source given. — [EasySell blog](https://easysellapp.com/blogs/wiki/bangladesh-ecommerce-bkash-mobile-money-cod-shopify-market-entry-2026) [snippet]
- Old official data point: Evaly delivered 35.45 lakh of 1.086 crore orders (32.98%) between Jan 2020 and Jan 2021. This is a non-delivery case, not a refusal rate. — [TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/node/230902) [snippet]
- Pathao Courier: from Tk 60 inside Dhaka, Tk 25 per kg above 1 kg, COD charge 0.5% for Dhaka-to-Dhaka (help page) or 1% (blog). — [Pathao help](https://help.pathao.com/?p=1725); [Pathao blog](https://pathao.com/?p=7787) [snippet]
- Steadfast: Tk 65 up to 500 g inside Dhaka per a third-party comparison (not verified on Steadfast's site). Steadfast's terms say it does not charge for return parcels and that rates can be customised. — [quasa.io (Bangla)](https://quasa.io/bn/media/pathao-na-steadfast-bdt-5-kam-nay-ritarnei-asal-kharach-badalay); [Steadfast terms](https://steadfast.com.bd/terms-and-condition) [snippet]

### Inferences
- Tk 1,300 marketing per device is about US$10.45. If the blog CPC range were right, that budget buys about 100–350 clicks per sale, which looks generous. The real constraint is conversion on a Tk 15,000 unfamiliar product, and no data exists for it. Treat Tk 1,300 as untested.
- On COD, every refused parcel costs two-way courier fees and handling. At a 20–30% RTO rate (unverified), prepaid deposits or bKash advance payments would materially change the unit economics.

### Gaps
- No Bangladesh cost per purchase or cost per lead for Meta ads was found. Only a test campaign would give a reliable number.
- No official or courier-published COD refusal rate was found.
- Courier rates outside Dhaka were not retrieved.

## 7. Payments, tax and exchange rate

### Takeaway
Bangla QR merchant fees run about 1% (bKash about 1.15% on one checkout page). No official bKash/Nagad merchant fee schedule was found. Standard VAT is 15%. Imports carry customs duty by HS code plus regulatory duty, 15% VAT, about 7.5% advance tax and 5% advance income tax, so a non-concessional parts stack can exceed the plan's 20%. The taka was about Tk 124–126 per US$ in November 2026.

## 8. Hardware cost reality checks: BOM $52 at 1k units, $38 at 10k

### Takeaway
A $52 BOM at 1,000 units for camera + compute + audio + battery is tight but not unreasonable. Retail Raspberry Pi parts alone (Zero 2 W plus Camera Module 3 at $25) take up much of it. $38 at 10k is about what Amazon reached for the Echo Dot 2nd gen ($34.87) at mass scale, so it likely needs a custom board. ## 10. Hardware startup benchmarks: gross margins and failure reasons

### Takeaway
Public consumer-electronics companies run about 33–45% gross margin (median about 43%). The usual rule of thumb prices a device at 2.5–4× BOM. SenseBook at Tk 14,990 is about 2.3× a $52 BOM and about 3.2× a $38 BOM. The most-cited failure research (CB Insights, about 400 failed consumer hardware startups, 2017) points to lack of demand, high burn and failure to raise follow-on rounds.

### Cited Findings
- Consumer-electronics public comps: 33–45% gross margin, 43.4% median, ranging from GoPro 33.6% to Garmin 58.7%. Single-category DTC brands should plan for the low end. — [Eightx (CFO advisory blog)](https://eightx.co/blog/electronics-financial-benchmark) [snippet]
- Hardware gross margin is about 35%, against software's 80%+ (venture firm). A 30% GM is cited as the minimum to sustain DTC electronics growth. — [Bitkraft](https://bitkraft.vc/insights/the-hardware-gap); [Financial Models Lab](https://financialmodelslab.com/blogs/kpi-metrics/smart-helmet-manufacturing-company) [snippet; template site, low authority]
- Rule of thumb: charge 2.5–4× BOM in consumer electronics. — [TechSpot community post](https://www.techspot.com/community/topics/will-your-hardware-startup-make-money.218746/) [snippet; attribution from search summary, older post]
- Apple products gross margin 36.8% in FY2025. — [Rework](https://resources.rework.com/libraries/startup-fundamentals/hardware-startup-business-model) [snippet]
- Youdao smart-device gross margin was 38.1–50.3% across 2025 quarters. — [Youdao results](https://www.barchart.com/story/news/149225/youdao-reports-fourth-quarter-and-fiscal-year-2025-unaudited-financial-results) [snippet]
- CB Insights studied nearly 400 failed consumer hardware startups and found lack of consumer demand and high burn rate among the main causes. The median crowdfunding raise was $210K. About 70% of hardware startups die or become "zombies" after raising outside funding, because later rounds are hard to get (2017). — [CB Insights](https://www.cbinsights.com/research/report/hardware-startups-failure-success/?os=dio); [CNBC, 27 Sep 2017](https://www.cnbc.com/2017/09/27/juicero-and-jawbone-havent-put-early-stage-investors-off-hardware.html) [snippet]

### Inferences
- Rough unit margin at 1k units: VAT-inclusive Tk 14,990 gives about Tk 13,035 net. Minus BOM plus 20% duty (52 × 1.2 × 124.35 ≈ Tk 7,759) leaves about Tk 5,276, about 40% hardware gross margin. That is before assembly, freight, payment and COD fees (about Tk 225–325), courier, returns, warranty and Tk 1,300 marketing. Net contribution would fall to the low-20s %. If VAT is not included in the price, or duty is higher than 20%, the result moves noticeably.
- At about 2.3× BOM (at 1k units) the price is below the 2.5–4× rule. The plan relies on the subscription to make up the margin, and the subscription assumptions (45% attach, 60% retention) are unbenchmarked (see section 5).
- The CB Insights failure pattern matters here: Bangladesh follow-on funding is thin (US$6M in H1 2026, 100% foreign), so the risk of becoming a "zombie" after the seed round is higher than in the US benchmark.

- No published failure analysis of Bangladeshi hardware startups was found.
- The CB Insights hardware report is from 2017 and gated, so the full ranking was not read.
