# Short survey for parents (5 minutes)

**Use:** on paper, by phone or as an online form. **Sample:** 100–200 parents of Class 6–10 students, recruited through schools, coaching centres and parent groups in at least two cities. **No names or phone numbers on this form.** Contact details for the trial go on a separate consent sheet.

**Price rotation:** each form shows one price for questions 9 and 10: **A = Tk 9,990, B = Tk 12,990, C = Tk 14,990**. Print equal numbers of A, B and C forms and hand them out in turn. The app price in question 10 is a test value, not a plan.

The Bangla version below is a **draft translation**. Have a native speaker check it and test it with three parents before use.

> **The idea:** a small device that clips onto the top of an open textbook. It reads the page aloud, explains hard parts in Bangla or English when your child asks by voice, gives quizzes and reminds them what to revise. It has no screen and works with earphones. Parents get a weekly report and can choose a mode that gives hints instead of answers.

## How to read the results
- **Q5** is the need. Report the share answering 1 or more evenings, by city type and income band.
- **Q4** is the price anchor. SenseBook's price story ("less than four months of one tutor") only works for families in the Tk 2,000+ bands.
- **Q9** overstates real buying. As a rough rule, count only "Definitely" and halve it. Real deposits (parent guide, Part 5) beat any survey answer.
- **Q10** is the phone test: the share choosing the device over the app, at each price.
- Compare A, B and C to see how demand falls with price.
