# SenseBook validation kit

A low-cost way to test SenseBook with real people before building hardware at volume. It goes with the [validation report](../SenseBook_Validation_Report.md).

**Status:** draft, November 2026. Nothing in this kit has been used in the field yet. Run a dry run with 2–3 friendly people and fix the wording before real fieldwork.

## The three tests to run first
1. **The phone test.** Does a family pick a SenseBook over a free phone app on a stand after a week with each, and will they pay a deposit? (Guides 01 and 03, trial arms A and B, survey.)
2. **The Bangla reading test.** On real NCTB pages, photographed the way the device sees them, is reading accurate and is the voice pleasant enough to listen to for 30 minutes? (Bench tests T1 and T2 in 06.)
3. **The blind-reader and sponsor test.** Can blind readers attach it and read alone? Will at least two organisations commit in writing to fund units at the planned price? (Guides 02 and 04, trial arm C.)

## Who to talk to (minimum sample)
| Group | Number | Mix |
|---|---|---|
| Students, sighted | 12 | Class 6–10; Bangla and English versions; Dhaka plus one other city; at least 4 from families without a tutor |
| Blind and low-vision readers | 8 | At least 4 students in integrated or special schools; 2 low-vision adults; braille and non-braille users |
| Parents | 15 | Including the parents of the students above; at least 5 from middle-income homes |
| Teachers and school heads | 5 | 3 private schools, 1 government school, 1 integrated school for blind children |
| Organisations and funders | 5 | 2 blind-reader organisations, 1 NGO with an education programme, 2 CSR teams (telecom or bank) |

Interviews find the problems. The survey and the trial measure them. Don't treat 12 interviews as a percentage.

## Ethics and consent (do this every time)
- **Children:** get the guardian's written consent and the child's own spoken agreement. Interview children with a guardian nearby. Never alone in a closed room.
- **Blind participants:** read the consent form aloud, give an accessible copy (large print, braille or audio) and record spoken consent.
- **Recording:** audio only, and only with consent. Transcribe, then delete the audio within 30 days.
- **Data:** give each person a code (S01, B01, P01, T01, O01). Names, phone numbers and addresses stay on a paper consent sheet, locked away, and never go into the results sheet or the public repo.
- **Payment:** pay every participant for their time (suggested Tk 500 for an interview, Tk 2,000 for the one-week trial, plus travel). Pay blind co-designers as experts.
- **No pressure:** saying no, stopping or skipping a question is always fine, and the payment is the same.
- **Honesty:** say clearly that SenseBook is a prototype. Don't promise a delivery date, a price or that the participant will get a device.
