Specifications

Every new dimension, mass and source in the v2 model, read straight from cad/hw2_params.py (mm, degrees, grams). v1's own numbers stay in the build chat's sb_params.py at commit 598d0f3.

SenseBook hardware v2 proposal: every new dimension, mass and source in one place (mm, degrees, grams).

Built ON TOP of the build chat's v1 model (design/cad/sb_params.py and build_model.py, read-only).
Everything v1 already defines (outer shell 88 x 40 x 34 mm, stick, hub, blades, rear leg, buttons, side
controls, jack, USB-C, status dots, top microphones, cover screws, heat spreader) is reused unchanged.
This file only adds or replaces what v2 changes.

Frames are the v1 frames (see sb_params.py): UNIT frame for parts in the head (x across, +y toward the
reader, z up; front face y = 0, back y = -34), BOOK frame for the hub, stick and legs.
Facet-local coordinates (u, v, a) are v1's: u = x, v = up the lens facet, a = depth into the unit.

Sources (research notes in the session notes folder, 27 Nov 2026):
  [R1] compute_radio_power.md: RK3576 package, memory and radio module sizes and prices
  [R2] camera_audio_light_battery.md: camera FOV, speaker, sensors, LED driver, lens sizes
  [D1] design/cad/out/analysis_results.json: lens height and tip at the 9 cm stick (build chat)
EST = our estimate, not from a datasheet. Nothing has been built or measured.

Cameras (4 x 50 MP, stock phone-type AF modules)

Sensor: Samsung S5KJN1-class 50 MP, 8160 x 6144 px, 1/2.76" [R2]. Module size EST 8.5-9.5 mm square [R2]. All four cameras sit on v1's sloped lens facet (it already faces the page), in one row: far-left, near-left, ToF sensor, near-right, far-right. Facet coords (u, v); lens front at depth NEAR_A / FAR_A. Aims: build_v2.py tries every aim in 1 deg steps; within MAX_TILT it keeps the one with the highest worst-case dpi on the printed text, the flattest among near-equals. Facet normal = 55.5 deg below horizontal at the 9 cm stick [D1].

Cells: seven stepped pouch cells in parallel, in the space the Pi-era parts used

Stepped cells: an upper cell at full depth above the camera row, a thinner lower cell behind the cameras. The step height and the lower cells' front face are computed in build_v2.py from the camera modules (0.7 mm gap). Front cell: the block above the camera row and behind the front wall, free once every camera sits on the facet. x stops short of the USB-C sub-board (x 33); the top stays under the v1 top-button boards; z0 = the step height. [R2]: Grepow shaped cells up to 460 Wh/L; 4.45 V cells ~500-600 Wh/L for a small custom cell (EST, low end used); phones claim 805-850 Wh/L for silicon-carbon cells (small cells lose some of that to seals and tabs).
