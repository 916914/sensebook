## Device description (paste first, every time)
SenseBook is a tiny, screen-free AI reading companion that sits at the top of an open book, exactly centred on the spine. It has three parts:

1. **The unit:** a horizontal rounded-rectangle module the size of a webcam (about 8.5 × 3.8 × 3 cm), in matte deep slate-teal. On the upright upper part of its front face it has four small white dots in a row at the upper left. The bottom of the front face is a sloped facet that angles downward, and one round camera lens with a pale blue glass ring and a thin soft light ring sits on it. **The lens points down at the open pages, not straight ahead;** the unit tips slightly forward. A small concave marigold "Ask" button sits on top.
2. **The stick:** a very short, slim stick only about 9 cm tall (it can pull up taller when needed), ending in a small hub. The unit sits low, just a hand's width above the top of the book.
3. **The tri-grip:** three very thin legs spread from the hub, like a miniature tripod.
   - The two front legs are wafer-thin flat metal blades that slide down inside the book's front and back covers, one on each side of the spine, hidden under the pages.
   - The third leg reaches behind the book and presses gently on the back of the spine with a small soft pad.
   - Together the three legs hug the book, keeping the device centred at the top of the spine.

Nothing touches the pages. The look is minimal, modern and premium, with soft rounded corners. No board, no base, no clamp, no magnets, no screen. No text, no logos.

## If the image drifts
- Lens looking straight ahead: add "the lens is on a downward-sloping facet and points down at the pages; the unit tips forward".
- Thick or chunky legs: add "the legs are wafer-thin like flat spring-steel strips, barely visible".
- Something on the pages: add "the two blades are hidden inside the covers, under the pages; nothing touches the pages".
- Not centred: add "the hub sits exactly over the spine; the unit is centred over the spine".
