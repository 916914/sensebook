## Step 2: scene prompts (written for Option A; swap in your chosen stand)
## Product description (paste first, every time)
SenseBook is a small, screen-free AI reading companion that stands at the top of an open book.

The main unit is a compact horizontal rounded-rectangle module, about the size of a webcam (8.5 × 3.8 × 3 cm), in a deep slate-teal matte finish. On its front face it has four small white dots in a row near the upper left (status lights and speaker) and one round camera lens with a pale blue glass ring at the lower centre. A thin soft light ring circles the lens.

The module sits on a short, slim stick (about 20–25 cm), centred under it. At the bottom of the stick is a thin flat metal plate that slides under the top edge of the open book at the spine, so the book's own weight holds the device steady. The plate is barely visible under the book, and nothing touches or clips the pages. The module sits just above and behind the top edge of the book, centred on the spine, with the lens angled down toward the reader so it sees both pages.

On top of the module is one small concave round "Ask" button in warm marigold. Small volume buttons are on the side, and a 3.5 mm headphone jack is on the side.

The look is minimal, modern and premium, with soft rounded corners: calm, not a toy, not a robot. Not a desk lamp. There is no clip on the pages, no base on the table and no long arm. No screen. No text, no logos.

## If the image drifts
- Lamp-like or too big: add "the module is tiny, the size of a webcam; there is no base on the table and no long arm".
- Clipped to the pages: add "nothing touches the pages; a thin plate is tucked under the book's top edge".
- Lens in the wrong place: add "one round lens at the lower centre of the module's front face, angled down at the pages".
- For a consistent set, make image 1 first, then start later prompts with "Same device as the previous image."
