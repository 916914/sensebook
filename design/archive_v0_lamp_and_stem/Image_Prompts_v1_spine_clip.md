# SenseBook: image prompts for every scenario (spine-clip concept)

This goes with `SenseBook_Attachment_Plan.md`. Paste the **device description** first, then one scenario. Make scenario 1 first, then start the others with "Same device as the previous image" to keep it consistent. Image models draw Bangla text badly, so add text later in Canva or Figma.

## Device description (paste first, every time)
SenseBook is a tiny, screen-free AI reading companion that rides on the top of an open book, at the spine. It has three parts:

1. **The unit:** a horizontal rounded-rectangle module the size of a webcam (about 8.5 × 3.8 × 3 cm), in matte deep slate-teal. On its front face it has four small white dots in a row at the upper left and one round camera lens with a pale blue glass ring at the lower centre, with a thin soft light ring around the lens. A small concave marigold "Ask" button sits on top.
2. **The stick:** a short, slim stick about 15 cm tall under the unit.
3. **The clip:** a small spring clip at the bottom of the stick that grips the top edge of the book's cover right beside the spine. The clip's thin inner jaw slides between the cover and the first page, so it sits hidden underneath the pages. Nothing clips or touches the pages themselves.

The unit sits just behind the top edge of the book, centred over the spine, never over the pages, with its lens angled down toward the reader so it sees both pages. The look is minimal, modern and premium, with soft rounded corners. Not a desk lamp, no base on the table, no long arm, no screen. No text, no logos.

## If the image drifts
- Clip on the pages: add "the clip grips only the cover, hidden under the pages; nothing touches the pages".
- Unit hanging over the pages: add "the unit sits just behind the top edge of the book, never over the pages".
- Too big or lamp-like: add "the unit is the size of a webcam; the stick is short and slim; there is no base".
- Lens in the wrong place: add "one round lens at the lower centre of the unit's front face, angled down at the pages".
