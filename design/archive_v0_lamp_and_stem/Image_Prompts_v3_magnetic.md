## Device description (paste first, every time)
SenseBook is a tiny, screen-free AI reading companion that rides on the top of any open book, at the spine. It has three parts:

1. **The unit:** a horizontal rounded-rectangle module the size of a webcam (about 8.5 × 3.8 × 3 cm), in matte deep slate-teal. On the upright upper part of its front face it has four small white dots in a row at the upper left. The bottom of the front face is a sloped facet that angles downward, and one round camera lens with a pale blue glass ring and a thin soft light ring sits on that facet. **The lens points down at the open pages, not straight ahead.** The whole unit also tips slightly forward on its stick, looking down at the book. A small concave marigold "Ask" button sits on top.
2. **The stick:** a short, slim stick about 15 cm tall.
3. **The mount:** a slim fold-over mount, made of two thin rounded slate-teal plates joined by a flexible hinge strip. It folds over the top edge of the book's cover right beside the spine. One plate sits on the outside of the cover, and the other slides inside, between the cover and the first page, hidden under the pages. Hidden magnets hold the plates together through the cover. Nothing pinches or touches the pages.

The unit sits just behind the top edge of the book, centred over the spine, never over the pages, with its lens angled down toward the reader so it sees both pages. The look is minimal, modern and premium, with soft rounded corners. No board, no base, no long arm, no screen. No text, no logos.

## If the image drifts
- Lens looking straight ahead: add "the lens is on a downward-sloping facet at the bottom of the unit's front and points down at the pages; the unit tips forward".
- Big clip or clamp: add "the mount is two thin flat plates joined by a soft hinge, held by magnets; no spring clip".
- Something on the pages: add "the inner plate is hidden under the pages; nothing touches the pages".
- Unit over the pages: add "the unit sits just behind the top edge of the book, never over the pages".
- Too big: add "the unit is the size of a webcam; the stick is short and slim; no board, no base".
