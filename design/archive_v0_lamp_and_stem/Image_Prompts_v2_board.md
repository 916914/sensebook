# SenseBook: image prompts, universal reading-board design (v2)

Paste the **device description** first, then one scenario. Make scenario 1 first, then begin the others with "Same device as the previous image". Image models draw Bangla text badly, so add text later.

## Device description (paste first, every time)
SenseBook is a tiny, screen-free AI reading companion that sits at the top of any open book, centred on the spine. It has three parts:

1. **The unit:** a horizontal rounded-rectangle module the size of a webcam (about 8.5 × 3.8 × 3 cm), in matte deep slate-teal. On its front face it has four small white dots in a row at the upper left and one round camera lens with a pale blue glass ring at the lower centre, with a thin soft light ring around the lens. A small concave marigold "Ask" button sits on top.
2. **The stick:** a short, slim stick about 15 cm tall that rises from the top centre of the board.
3. **The reading board:** a thin, slate-teal fold-out board about 26 × 20 cm and 3 mm thick, with a soft grippy surface and a low raised edge along its top. The open book simply rests on the board with its top edge against that raised edge. Nothing clips or grips the book or its pages.

The unit sits just behind the top edge of the book, never over the pages, with its lens angled down toward the reader so it sees both pages. The look is minimal, modern and premium, with soft rounded corners. Not a desk lamp, no long arm, no screen. No text, no logos.

## If the image drifts
- Something gripping the book: add "nothing clips the book; it simply rests on the board".
- Unit over the pages: add "the unit sits just behind the top edge of the book, never over the pages".
- Board too big or thick: add "the board is thin like a clipboard and only a little wider than the middle of the book".
