# Name check: "SenseBook"

Checked on 27 November 2026 from public sources. This is a desk search, not legal advice. Before filing or printing packaging, a registered trademark agent in Bangladesh must run a full search at the Department of Patents, Designs and Trademarks (DPDT). DPDT has no public online search that we could use.

## What we found

| Where | Finding | Risk to us |
|---|---|---|
| US trademark register (USPTO) | **SENSEBOOK**, owner Holtz Office Support GmbH, Class 16 (paper goods). Registered 2013, **cancelled 15 May 2023** (serial 79131043, reg. 4457167). | None now. The mark is dead. |
| US trademark register (USPTO) | **SENSEEBOOK**, owner Sensee, Inc. (Daejeon, South Korea). **Live, registered 19 Aug 2025** (serial 97840276, reg. 7895916). Classes 35, 40 and 41: braille book printing and publishing, e-books in the field of braille books, libraries, book retail. Sensee makes braille transcription tools for visually impaired readers. | **High abroad.** Almost the same sound, in exactly our Access field (books for blind readers). It could block or oppose a US or international filing in Class 41 and confuse partners in the global blindness sector. |
| sensebook.app | A live Russian-language product called **SenseBook**, described as a "living interactive book" with personal dialogue and self-reflection questions. | Medium. Same name, a similar idea (a book you talk with), different market and language. |
| Domains (RDAP, 27 Nov) | sensebook.com (parked), sensebook.app (above), sensebook.org: taken. sensebook.io, sensebook.ai, sensebook.net, sensebook.com.bd: free. | Medium. No .com. We can use sensebook.com.bd and sensebook.io. |
| Google Play (Bangladesh store) | No app called SenseBook. Nearest: "Common Sense - Book", "Sense: Shared Family Calendar". | Low. |
| "-book" names | Facebook sued Teachbook.com in 2010 to stop it using "book" in its name; in 2011 the court refused to dismiss the case. | Low for a reading device, but Meta has defended the suffix before. |
| Older press | "SenseBooks": printed books with built-in sensors (children's, music and cookery books), covered years ago in the Guardian; current status unknown. | Low. |
| Bangladesh (DPDT) | Not checked: no public online search. | Unknown. Must be checked before filing. |

Not found: any reading device, tutor or education app called SenseBook in Bangladesh or India.

## Decision (owner, 27 Nov 2026)

**Keep "SenseBook".** Next steps: a registered agent runs a DPDT search this month, then file "SenseBook" and "সেন্সবুক" in Classes 9 and 41 in Bangladesh. Revisit only if that search, or a lawyer's view on SENSEEBOOK before export, finds a conflict.

## Recommendation (as put to the owner)

**Keep "SenseBook" for now, and decide for good after a Bangladesh trademark search this month.**

Reasons to keep it:
- No live identical mark found, and no SenseBook product in Bangladesh.
- The name is already on the repository, hub site, plan, spec, software and the wake phrase "শোনো সেন্সবুক".
- It says what the product does in English: it senses the page and works with a book.

What would make us change: if the DPDT search finds a conflicting mark, or if a lawyer advises that SENSEEBOOK blocks our Class 9 and 41 filings abroad. We plan to export in Year 3, so the Sensee mark matters later even if Bangladesh is clear.

The brand system is built so the name can change cheaply: the symbol carries no letters, and the wordmark is one file.

## Two alternatives, if the owner prefers a Bangla name

| | **Kothaboi** (কথাবই) | **Pathsathi** (পাঠসাথী) |
|---|---|---|
| Meaning | "Talking book": কথা (talk) + বই (book). It is the headline "বই এখন কথা বলে" in one word, and "talking book" is a term blind readers already know. | "Study companion": পাঠ (lesson, reading) + সাথী (companion). Says the tutor role directly. |
| Clashes found | None in web search, Google Play or the USPTO results we saw. "Kotha" is the name of an unrelated Bangladeshi social app. | None found. |
| Domains (27 Nov) | kothaboi.com, .app, .io, .com.bd all free. | pathsathi.com, .app, .io, .com.bd all free. |
| For | Short, warm, Bangla-first, easy to own, easy to say in English (KO-tha-boy). | Clear promise; serious, education-first tone for parents and schools. |
| Against | Could be heard as "audiobook" only, not tutoring. | Longer; English readers may read "path". Less distinctive. |
