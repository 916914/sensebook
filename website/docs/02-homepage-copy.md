## 3. Our first chapter

**SenseBook** (brief)

The first invention in our mission to expand human potential. (brief)

SenseBook transforms physical books into conversational learning experiences, helping people understand, question and explore knowledge more deeply. (brief)

It clips onto a printed book, reads the page, and answers questions aloud. There is no screen to look at, only the book.

Status line: In development. Images are renders of the current prototype design.

Link: **Explore SenseBook**

Visual: render 01 (the unit over an open book, studio light).

## 4. Why it matters

**Understanding should not depend on who is in the room.**

**The student seeking understanding.** A lesson moves on before an idea has landed. With SenseBook, a student can ask about the paragraph in front of them and hear it explained again, another way, for as long as it takes.

**The university learner exploring complex ideas.** Difficult texts reward questions. A reader can stop at a hard passage, test their reading of it, and follow a thought further than the page goes.

**The blind reader pursuing independence.** Print should not require a second pair of eyes. SenseBook reads the page aloud and answers questions about it, with physical controls made to be used by touch.

**The lifelong learner expanding capability.** Curiosity does not end with school. For someone learning a new field or a new trade, any book on the shelf can become a patient conversation.
